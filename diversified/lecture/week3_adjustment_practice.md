# Week 3 — 교정 실무 (Adjustment Practice)

> 분석을 **손기술**로 전환 — 분절별 · 관절별 핵심 기법
> Week 2의 평가 결과를 Diversified HVLA의 실제 **추력(thrust)**으로 연결

---

## 🎯 이 주차의 학습 목표

- **1.** HVLA 추력의 **3대 요소**(자세·접촉점·벡터)를 분해 설명할 수 있다
- **2.** **Pre-load tension → Elastic barrier → Thrust** 연속 절차를 수행할 수 있다
- **3.** 경추·흉추·요추·골반·말초 관절별 **대표 기법 20+**를 시연 수준으로 이해한다
- **4.** 술자 **자기 신체관리**(biomechanics)로 손·손목 부상을 예방할 수 있다

## 제1부. 교정술의 3대 요소 {#part1}

### 1.1 Three Elements of Adjustment

모든 Diversified HVLA는 세 요소의 조합:

```
① 환자 자세 (Position)
    → 병변 분절을 접근 가능한 상태로
② 접촉점 (Contact Point)
    → 정확한 해부학적 지점에 표면적 최소 (thumb/pisiform/index)
③ 추력 벡터 (Line of Drive, LOD)
    → 방향 · 각도 · 속도 · 깊이
```

### 1.2 각 요소 세부

#### ① 환자 자세
- Supine (앙와위), Prone (복와위), Side Posture (측와위), Seated (좌위), Standing (기립)
- 중력·체중 활용 + 분절 **pre-tension** 만들기 용이한 자세 선택
- 환자 편안함 · 안정성 · 접근성

#### ② 접촉점
| 접촉 도구 | 표면적 | 사용 분절 |
|-----------|--------|-----------|
| **Thumb** | 작음 · 고정밀 | 경추 transverse process · mamillary |
| **Index MCP** | 중간 | C1 TP · 중·하부 경추 |
| **Pisiform** | 중간 · 안정 | 흉추 TP · 요추 mamillary · 골반 PSIS |
| **Hypothenar** | 넓음 · 파워 | 흉추 양측 TP · sacrum |
| **Web (엄지-검지 사이)** | 넓음 | Cervical (아래쪽) |

#### ③ 추력 벡터 (LOD)
- **방향** — Posterior-Anterior / Medial-Lateral / Superior-Inferior 조합
- **각도** — 관절 면 따라 (facet orientation)
- **속도** — 100-150ms (HV = high velocity)
- **깊이** — 1-3mm (LA = low amplitude)

### 1.3 HVLA의 물리학 원칙

**공식**: Force = Mass × Acceleration  
**임상적으로**: Low amplitude + High velocity = 높은 가속도 → 단시간 내 cavitation 유도 + 환자 무의식 방어(guarding) 최소화.

### 1.4 Set-up의 중요성

경험상 교정 결과의 **70%는 set-up** (자세·접촉·pre-tension)에 달려 있음. **30%가 thrust 자체**. 초보자는 thrust에 집중하지만, 숙련자는 set-up에 시간 투자.

## 제2부. Pre-load Tension과 Elastic Barrier 감각 훈련 {#part2}

### 2.1 Joint Motion의 범위 구분

```
Active range    Passive range    Paraphysiological space
┌────────────┬────────────────┬─────────────────┐
│            │                │   <-- HVLA target
│ 환자 자력   │  검사자 유도    │   Cavitation zone
└────────────┴────────────────┴─────────────────┘
                              ↑               ↑
                      Elastic barrier    Anatomical limit
                      (= end feel)       (손상 위험)
```

### 2.2 Set-up 절차 (표준 HVLA)

1. **환자 자세 완성** (전신 긴장 제거 확인)
2. **접촉점** 정확 위치 — 환자 호흡 (들숨 or 날숨 끝)
3. **Slack 제거** — 관절이 passive range 끝에 도달할 때까지 부드럽게
4. **Pre-load tension** 형성 — "tension on tissue"
5. **Elastic barrier 감각** — 검사자가 느끼는 "부드러운 저항 벽"
6. **Thrust** — HVLA thrust (100-150ms, 1-3mm)
7. **Cavitation 소리** (audible release, 80% 성공 시)
8. **Release** — 검사자 압력 즉시 해제
9. **Re-assessment** — Motion palpation 재확인

### 2.3 학습자 훈련 팁

- **Slack 제거**는 가장 어려운 단계 — 반복 훈련 필요
- 처음 3개월: 정확한 **elastic barrier 감각** 찾기에만 집중
- "딱" 소리 연연 말기 — 소리 없어도 관절 변화 가능
- 매일 10분 셀프 palpation 훈련 권장

### 2.4 Thrust Type

| Type | 설명 | 대표 적용 |
|------|------|-----------|
| **Recoil** | 빠른 접촉 후 즉시 손 떼기 | Atlas (Toggle-style) |
| **Impulse** | 한 번의 short thrust | 경추 chair 기법 |
| **Graded** | 점진적 force | Thoracic drop piece 동반 |
| **Rotary break** | 회전 성분 thrust | 경추 회전 기법 |

### 3.1 후두골 (Occiput · C0) 리스팅 {#c0}

C0 occiput 리스팅은 **6축 조합**(PS/PI/AS/AI/RS/LS)으로 세분되며 접촉점과 LOD가 각각 다릅니다. States Manual은 다음 9개 술식을 수록합니다.

---

#### 3.1.1 Posterior Superior Occiput

![Posterior superior occiput — full view (Fig. 34A)](../images/states_manual/p37_posterior_superior_occiput_A.png)
![Posterior superior occiput — close-up view (Fig. 34B)](../images/states_manual/p37_posterior_superior_occiput_B.png)

**원문 (p. 37)**
```
L:   RPS–LPS occiput.
PP:  Supine; headpiece lowered; head rotated 90 degrees (or to maximum rotation
     for the patient) with listing up.
DP:  Homolateral to listing; toggle stance; at right angles to patient, facing
     towards patient.
CH:  Caudad hand; palmar contact over cheek and mandible; thumb posterior to
     ear with pad of thumb on the posteroinferior aspect of mastoid; fifth digit
     inferior to jaw; forearm parallel and close to sternum (Fig. 34B).
IH:  Cephalad hand; cup ear with palm, second digit anterior to SCM, third digit
     posterior to SCM, with forearm parallel to the floor (#1 indifferent hand
     contact) (Fig. 24A).
T:   Traction cephalad with CH and IH. The IH laterally flexes between occiput
     and atlas homolateral to listing. CH then induces quick rotation to occiput
     through mastoid contact.
```

**한국어 요점**
- **리스팅**: 좌/우 후상방 후두골 편위 (RPS = 우측 후상방)
- **핵심**: 머리 90° 회전으로 병변측 위로 올린 후, IH의 측굴 + CH의 유양돌기 접촉 회전 thrust
- **주의**: 측굴은 반드시 C0–C1 사이에서만. SCM 접촉은 감각적으로만 지시, 압박 아님

#### 3.1.2 Anterior Occiput

![Anterior occiput — full view (Fig. 35A)](../images/states_manual/p38_anterior_occiput_A.png)
![Anterior occiput — close-up view (Fig. 35B)](../images/states_manual/p38_anterior_occiput_B.png)

**원문 (p. 38)**
```
L:   RPS–LPS occiput (변형).
PP:  Supine; headpiece lowered; head rotated 90 degrees with listing up.
DP:  Homolateral to listing, toggle stance facing caudad, 45 degrees to
     midsagittal plane of patient.
CH:  Calcaneal-hypothenar contact on zygomatic arch and mandible homolateral
     to listing; fingers lie cephalad on body of mandible.
     Avoid contact with the temporomandibular joint.
IH:  Cup ear with palm; fingers wrap around base of occiput and upper cervical
     spine (#1 indifferent hand contact).
T:   CH drives quickly in an obliquely caudad direction, in line with body of
     mandible, toward opposite shoulder. IH laterally flexes between occiput
     and atlas homolateral to listing just prior to thrust.
```

**한국어 요점**
- PSO와 접촉 부위가 다름 — 협골궁·하악골에 calcaneal-hypothenar
- **TMJ 접촉 금지** (턱관절 외상 위험)
- Thrust 방향: 반대측 어깨를 향한 사선 caudad

#### 3.1.3 Superior Condyle ⭐

![Superior condyle (Fig. 36)](../images/states_manual/p39_superior_condyle.png)

**원문 (p. 39)**
```
L:   RLS–LL occiput.
PP:  Supine; headpiece neutral; patient's head in a neutral position.
DP:  Homolateral to listing; at right angle to patient with feet wider than
     shoulder width apart (toggle stance), facing patient at level of patient's
     shoulders.
CH:  Caudad hand; index contact on lateral aspect of mastoid process; remove
     skin slack from anterior to posterior. Forearm of CH is parallel to floor;
     wrist in ulnar deviation.
IH:  Cephalad hand; contact chin with fingers; forearm contacts lateral aspect
     of face and head (#2 indifferent hand contact) (Fig. 24B).
T:   IH tractions cephalad and laterally flexes over contact (homolateral to
     listing); CH thrusts through mastoid contact at a right angle to the
     midsagittal plane of the head (from mastoid to mastoid).
```

**한국어 요점**
- 사용자께서 예로 드신 기법. **중립 자세**(회전 없음)에서 수행
- CH는 유양돌기 외측면을 index로, 전→후로 skin slack 제거
- Thrust: mastoid to mastoid 직선(중시상면 수직)

#### 3.1.4 Suboccipital Lift

![Suboccipital lift (Fig. 37)](../images/states_manual/p40_suboccipital_lift.png)

> 원문 페이지(p. 40)는 스캔이 기울어져 일부 판독 불가. 핵심만 한국어로 요약.

**한국어 요점**
- **리스팅**: AI occiput (양측 후두하 긴장, 만성 두통)
- **자세**: Supine, 검사자 양손을 후두하근 아래로 삽입
- **기법**: 지속적 cephalad traction — thrust 없는 *sustained release*
- **용도**: 급성 경부통 후 suboccipital release, 경추 arthrosis 환자

#### 3.1.5 Bilateral Thenar Occiput

![Bilateral thenar occiput (Fig. 38)](../images/states_manual/p41_bilateral_thenar_occiput.png)

**원문 (p. 41)**
```
L:   AI occiput.  (NOTE: Useful for patients with cervical arthrosis.)
PP:  Prone antigravity; headpiece lowered; flex patient's occiput on upper
     cervical spine (tuck chin in).
DP:  At side of table, at level of patient's pelvis, facing cephalad in
     fencer's stance, with doctor's sternum over patient's midline.
CH:  Left thenar eminence medial to mastoid on left occipital base;
     thumb directed cephalad.
IH:  Right thenar eminence medial to mastoid on right occipital base;
     thumb directed cephalad.
T:   Apply traction through contact on occiput in a cephalad and anterior
     direction for 30–60 sec. Optional light thrust in same direction as
     traction at end of procedure. NO thrust with cervical arthrosis patient.
CONTRAINDICATION: Patients wearing hard contact lenses should remove
them prior to procedure.
```

**한국어 요점**
- **경추 arthrosis 환자에 유용** — thrust 없이 sustained traction만
- **경성 콘택트렌즈 착용자**: 안구 압박 위험 → 술전 제거 요청

#### 3.1.6 Lateral Occiput

![Lateral occiput (Fig. 39)](../images/states_manual/p42_lateral_occiput.png)

**원문 (p. 42, 일부 판독 보정)**
```
L:   RL–LL occiput.
PP:  Supine; headpiece lowered. Head rotated 90 degrees away from doctor
     with listing up.
DP:  Homolateral to side; toggle stance at right angles to patient.
CH:  Caudad hand; index contact on zygomatic arch, fingers pointing towards
     face; forearm at right angle to patient. NOTE: Make sure contact is NOT
     on TMJ.
IH:  Cephalad hand; #1 indifferent hand contact — cup ear with palm,
     chiropractic index contact; other fingers wrap around occiput.
T:   IH induces lateral flexion of occiput on upper cervicals as CH drives
     downward in line with patient's eyes; common break, scoop action.
CONTRAINDICATION: Patient cannot rotate head 90 degrees.
```

**한국어 요점**
- 외측편위(L/RL) occiput 전용
- "Scoop action" — 환자 눈 라인 방향으로 CH가 부드럽게 말아 내리는 thrust
- TMJ 접촉 절대 금지

#### 3.1.7 AI Occiput (Anterior Inferior)

![AI occiput (Fig. 40)](../images/states_manual/p43_ai_occiput.png)

**원문 (p. 43)**
```
L:   AI occiput; adjustment is done bilaterally; RAI–LAI occiput, adjustment
     is done on listed side only; posterior atlanto-occipital jamming;
     hyperextension whiplash. Patient presents with chin up; occiput low
     bilaterally or unilaterally; atlanto-occipital space decreased. Patient
     has difficulty dropping chin to chest; may have suboccipital muscle
     tension when dropping chin to chest.
PP:  Supine; headpiece lowered; head rotated 90 degrees away from doctor
     with listing up.
DP:  On homolateral side; toggle stance at right angles to patient.
CH:  Caudad hand; index contact on mastoid with rest of index finger on
     inferior margin of occiput; forearm in midclavicular line.
     No flexion or extension of wrist.
IH:  Cephalad hand; #2 indifferent hand contact; cups chin with fingers,
     forearm supports head.
T:   IH initially tractions cephalad, then tucks the chin as CH drives
     cephalad, lifting occiput.
CONTRAINDICATION: DO NOT use in acute phase of whiplash injury. Patient
most likely will not have full 90 degrees of cervical rotation. Use a soft
collar to immobilize neck until symptoms subside.
```

**한국어 요점**
- **제시 소견**: 턱이 올라간 자세, chin-to-chest 불가, 후두하근 긴장
- **hyperextension whiplash** 만성기 — **급성기엔 금기**
- IH는 턱을 올리며 견인, CH는 occiput 들어올림

#### 3.1.8 PI Occiput (Posterior Inferior)

![PI occiput (Fig. 41)](../images/states_manual/p44_pi_occiput.png)

**원문 (p. 44)**
```
L:   PI occiput; adjustment is done bilaterally; RPI–LPI occiput, adjustment
     is done on listed side only; anterior atlanto-occipital jamming;
     hyperflexion whiplash. Patient presents with chin down; occiput is high
     bilaterally or unilaterally; atlanto-occipital space increased. Patient
     easily drops chin to chest but has difficulty extending from a flexed
     position. Anterior muscles are tense while suboccipital muscles are
     relaxed.
PP:  Supine; headpiece lowered. Head rotated 90 degrees away from doctor
     with listing up.
DP:  On homolateral side; toggle stance at right angles to patient.
CH:  Caudad hand; index contact on posterior inferior mastoid process;
     forearm at right angles to patient, parallel to floor.
     No flexion or extension of wrist.
IH:  Cephalad hand; #2 indifferent hand contact; cup chin with fingers;
     forearm supports head.
T:   IH initially tractions cephalad, then lifts chin up as CH drives
     straight across in line with patient's eyes.
CONTRAINDICATION: DO NOT USE in acute phase of hyperflexion whiplash
injury. Patient most likely will not have full 90 degrees of cervical
rotation. Use a soft collar to immobilize neck until symptoms subside.
```

**한국어 요점**
- AI와 거울상 — **제시 소견**: 턱이 내려간 자세, 신전 불가, 전경근 긴장
- **hyperflexion whiplash** 만성기용, 급성기엔 금기
- CH Thrust: "환자 눈 라인 방향" 수평

#### 3.1.9 Two Hand Cervical Extension: Hyper- and Hypolordosis

![Two hand cervical extension — hyperlordosis (Fig. 48A)](../images/states_manual/p51_two_hand_cervical_extension_A.png)
![Two hand cervical extension — hypolordosis (Fig. 48B)](../images/states_manual/p51_two_hand_cervical_extension_B.png)

**원문 (p. 51, 일부 판독 보정)**
```
[Hyperlordosis]
L:   Hyperlordosis of cervical spine (Fig. 48A).
PP:  Supine; headpiece elevated.
DP:  At head of table, facing caudad in fencer's stance.
CH:  Fingers under occiput, contacting the superior nuchal line; thenar
     eminence and pad of thumb contact angle of mandible; palmar surface
     of hand cups face.
IH:  Same as CH (contralateral side).
T:   Flex head in obliquely anterior and cephalad direction, flattening
     cervical lordosis, for 45–60 sec.

[Hypolordosis]
L:   Hypolordosis of cervical spine (Fig. 48B).
PP:  Supine; headpiece level.
DP:  Head of table, facing caudad in toggle stance.
CH/IH: Second or third digit contacts posterior surface of AP of C4
     (apex of cervical lordosis); thenar eminence and pad of thumb
     contact angle of mandible; palmar surface of hand cups face.
T:   Remove skin slack over contact from medial to lateral; apply
     traction in a posterior to anterior direction through AP contact,
     introducing a lordotic curve to the cervical spine, for 45–60 sec.
     Release traction slowly.
```

**한국어 요점**
- Thrust 아닌 **sustained 자세 교정** (45–60초)
- Hyperlordosis: 곡선 **감소**시키는 굴곡 유지
- Hypolordosis: C4 후방 접촉으로 **곡선 복원** P→A 견인

### 3.2 환추 (Atlas · C1) 리스팅 {#c1}

Atlas는 가장 민감한 분절. States Manual은 TP laterality 교정용 3개 술식을 수록합니다.

---

#### 3.2.1 Lateral Atlas Index

![Lateral atlas index (Fig. 49)](../images/states_manual/p52_lateral_atlas_index.png)

**원문 (p. 52)**
```
L:   RI–LI, especially children and small adults.
PP:  Supine; head remains on level headpiece. Nose straight up.
DP:  On homolateral side; on level with patient's head; broad toggle stance
     at right angles to patient.
CH:  Caudad hand; index contact on apex of TP of atlas; skin slack taken out
     anterior to posterior; forearm parallel to floor; wrist in ulnar
     deviation with no flexion or extension of wrist.
IH:  Cephalad hand; #1 indifferent hand contact; cups ear with palm,
     chiropractic index and index fingers split SCM. If traction is needed
     can use a #2 indifferent hand contact; fingers cup chin, forearm
     supports head.
T:   IH induces lateral flexion. CH drives straight across in line with
     patient's eyes.
```

**한국어 요점**
- **소아·소체형 성인** 1차 선택
- 머리 중립 유지 — 회전 없음
- Thrust: 환자 눈 라인 수평 직선

---

#### 3.2.2 Lateral Atlas Pisiform

![Lateral atlas pisiform — initial hand contact (Fig. 50A)](../images/states_manual/p53_lateral_atlas_pisiform_A.png)
![Lateral atlas pisiform — final hand contact (Fig. 50B)](../images/states_manual/p53_lateral_atlas_pisiform_B.png)

**원문 (p. 53)**
```
L:   RI–LI atlas; technic of choice for mesomorphic or large patients.
PP:  Supine; headpiece lowered. Head rotated 90 degrees away from doctor
     with listing up.
DP:  On homolateral side; toggle stance at right angles to patient.
CH:  Caudad hand; pisiform contact on apex of TP of atlas; fifth digit
     below mandible; contact arm straight (Fig. 50B).
IH:  Cephalad hand; #1 contact; cup ear with palm, fingers wrap around
     occiput.
T:   IH applies cephalad traction and moves up on head as contact arm
     straightens. CH applies downward traction and then thrusts straight
     down.
CONTRAINDICATIONS: Patient cannot rotate head full 90 degrees; cervical
arthritis and/or osteoporosis.
NOTE: Skin slack is removed by placing pisiform on TP of atlas with fingers
pointing towards vertex of head (Fig. 50A); pisiform contact rotates so
little finger ends up just beneath the mandible. As hand is rotating, the
CH arm is straightening and IH is inducing lateral flexion. Do not flatten
hand so pisiform is lifted off contact and palm ends up on mandible.
```

**한국어 요점**
- **중등·대체형 성인** 1차 선택 (Index로 부족할 때)
- **Hand rotation sequence** 핵심 — 검지 → 새끼손가락이 하악 아래로 회전
- 금기: 경추 arthritis, 골다공증, 90° 회전 불가

---

#### 3.2.3 RI-LI Atlas (Posterior-Superior Contact)

![R I-LI atlas (Fig. 51)](../images/states_manual/p54_ri_li_atlas.png)

**원문 (p. 54, 스캔 일부 흐림 — 핵심만)**
```
L:   RI–LI atlas.
PP:  Supine; headpiece lowered; head rotated 90 degrees towards the doctor
     with listing up (laterality side down).
DP:  On homolateral side; toggle stance facing caudad at 45 degrees to
     patient.
CH:  Caudad hand; index contact on superior aspect of TP of atlas; take
     skin slack from A to P; wrist in ulnar deviation with no flexion or
     extension of wrist; contact arm straight.
IH:  Cephalad hand; cups ear, fingers wrap around occiput; forearm 45
     degrees to patient.
T:   IH initially flexes head; CH thrusts downward and posterior towards
     contralateral shoulder homolateral to contact.
```

**한국어 요점**
- Index · Pisiform 변형 — 머리를 **검사자 쪽으로** 회전 (반대 방향)
- Thrust 방향: 반대측 어깨로 대각 하방

---

### 3.3 C2 Axis 축추 {#c2}

States Manual 경추 섹션(pp. 37–54)에는 **C2 단독 전용 술식이 수록되어 있지 않습니다**. Axis는 아래 3.4의 **Rotary Cervical 계열**(C2–C7 공통)으로 교정하며, C2 PR/PL spinous listing은 thumb spinous push가 일반적입니다(Bergmann 3rd Ed Ch 5 참조 권장).

**실무 접근**:
- **C2 spinous PR/PL**: Supine, thumb contact on C2 spinous 외측, LOD = listing 반대 방향(PR → A+L). Rotary break 변형
- **C2 PP (posterior bilateral)**: Seated, thumb-index pincer on C2 spinous, P→A thrust
- 근거 교재: Bergmann TF, Peterson DH. *Chiropractic Technique* 3rd ed, Elsevier 2010, Ch 5

---

### 3.4 C3–C7 중·하부 경추 {#c3-c7}

States Manual은 C2–C7 공통 술식 6개를 수록합니다. 임상 선택은 **환자 체형·급/만성·신경증상 유무**에 따름.

---

#### 3.4.1 Rotary Cervical

![Rotary cervical (Fig. 42)](../images/states_manual/p45_rotary_cervical.png)

**원문 (p. 45, 일부 판독 보정)**
```
L:   RP–LP, C2 through C7.
PP:  Supine; headpiece neutral or raised slightly; head rotated with listing
     up.
DP:  Kneeling or standing, contralateral to side of listing, at level of
     patient's head; arms nearly parallel.
CH:  Caudad hand; second or third digit hooks on posterior surface of AP
     (distal palmar surface of second or third digit of listed segment);
     thumb anterior to ear, forearm angled at 45 degrees obliquely cephalad
     (in line with facets).
IH:  Cephalad hand; palm cups the lower ear, fingers contact occipital base
     (modified #1 indifferent hand contact).
T:   Prior to thrust, check to insure that no extension of cervicals is
     occurring; CH pulls quickly towards doctor, inducing a rotational
     thrust through the cervical spine. IH does not contribute to thrust.
```

**한국어 요점**
- **대표 rotation break 기법** (Diversified 경추의 기본)
- 전열(facet) 각도에 맞춰 45° 사선 견인
- **신전 금지** — 척추동맥 torsion 위험 증가

---

#### 3.4.2 Sitting Rotary Cervical

![Sitting rotary cervical (Fig. 43)](../images/states_manual/p46_sitting_rotary_cervical.png)

**원문 (p. 46)**
```
L:   RP–LP, C2–C7.
PP:  Seated on chair or table, seated erect.
DP:  Standing posterior or posterolateral to patient, contralateral to
     listing. Doctor's body supports patient's contralateral shoulder
     and torso.
CH:  Anterior hand (right hand when on patient's right side and vice versa);
     second or third digit hooks AP of listed vertebra, using distal palmar
     surface of digit. Palm cups ear; skin slack removed from P to A.
     Supported chiropractic index contact is also used (Fig. 23).
IH:  Posterior hand; palmar contact on contralateral mastoid process and
     occipital base; fingers directed cephalad.
T:   Traction cephalad with IH and CH; laterally flex cervical spine
     toward listed side and rotate to side opposite listing to the point
     of facet locking above contacted segment. Thrust quickly by pulling
     CH toward doctor inducing a rotational force through cervicals.
```

**한국어 요점**
- **좌위(Seated) 버전** — 임신부·고령·급성 요통 환자에 supine 부담 클 때
- Gonstead cervical chair 변형으로 응용 가능
- Facet locking까지 pre-load → rotational thrust

---

#### 3.4.3 Wrist Action Cervical Break

![Wrist action cervical break (Fig. 44)](../images/states_manual/p47_wrist_action_cervical_break.png)

**원문 (p. 47)**
```
L:   RP–LP, C2–C7.
PP:  Supine; head rotated with listing up, headpiece elevated.
DP:  Head of table facing caudad, knees into table, elbows held close
     together.
CH:  Homolateral hand; anterolateral aspect of DIP or PIP (either second
     or third digit) contact on listed AP; wrist held in ulnar deviation.
IH:  Contralateral hand; #1 indifferent hand contact; palmar aspect of
     hand supporting occiput, fingers supporting occiput, flexing head.
T:   High velocity wrist roll of CH only. IH is inactive during thrust.

Sequence:
1. Rotate head 45 degrees with listing up.
2. Place CH contact on AP; remove skin slack P to A.
3. Establish #1 IH contact. Thumbs should contact angle of mandible.
4. Flex occiput on atlas.
5. Flex cervical spine to include segment contacted.
6. Rotate head (listing up) to point of articular locking.
7. Extend upper cervicals.
8. Deliver fast wrist roll with CH only.
```

**한국어 요점**
- **Wrist-only thrust** — CH 손목 roll로 짧고 빠르게, 팔 전체 관여 최소화
- 8단계 sequence 정확 준수 — 각 단계 전환 시 Pre-load 재확인
- 숙련 후 선택 권장 (팔 전체 thrust보다 정밀)

---

#### 3.4.4 Calcaneal Cervical Break

![Calcaneal cervical break (Fig. 45)](../images/states_manual/p48_calcaneal_cervical_break.png)

**원문 (p. 48)**
```
L:   RP–LP, C2–C7; nonspecific for multiple listings.
PP:  Supine; headpiece neutral or elevated slightly, head rotated with
     listing up. Rotate to involve listed segment(s) in rotation; head
     rests on doctor's left hand in position of rotation only.
DP:  At side of table, homolateral to listing; kneeling or squatting,
     facing in toward patient at level of patient's head and shoulders.
CH:  Caudad hand; calcaneal contact on AP of involved segment(s)
     homolateral to listing, fingers directed cephalad and medial;
     elbow is positioned posterior and caudad in rotation to CH.
     Remove skin slack from P to A.
IH:  Cephalad hand; cup ear with palmar surface of IH (#1 IH contact),
     dorsum of hand rests on headpiece while supporting head.
T:   Thrust with CH only; driving away from doctor in an anterior and
     obliquely cephalad direction with a quick thrust. IH does not allow
     extension of head during set up or thrust.
```

**한국어 요점**
- **다중 segment 동시 교정**에 유리 (nonspecific)
- Hypothenar 대신 **calcaneal(손목 calcaneus)** 접촉으로 더 넓은 접촉면
- 신전 절대 금지 (IH가 고정)

---

#### 3.4.5 Cervical Stretch Acromion

![Cervical stretch acromion (Fig. 46)](../images/states_manual/p49_cervical_stretch_acromion.png)

**원문 (p. 49, 일부 판독 보정)**
```
L:   All listings (근긴장 이완 · 견인 목적).
PP:  Supine antigravity; headpiece closed and lowered slightly, head
     rotated to 90 degrees.
DP:  Kneeling at head of table, facing caudad. If head is turned to right,
     kneel on right knee; if to left, kneel on left knee.
CH:  Cephalad hand homolateral to head rotation; grasps chin; forearm
     contacts lateral aspect of face and occipital base.
IH:  Caudad hand contralateral to rotation; contacts superior aspect of
     homolateral shoulder, just medial to A-C joint. Use web between
     thumb and second digit.
T:   Traction cephalad with CH first; IH tractions caudad until resistance
     is met; CH tractions cephalad again and thrusts lightly directly
     cephalad. IH is inactive during thrust, maintaining caudad traction.
     Performed bilaterally.
```

**한국어 요점**
- **Pre-adjustment 근이완** 용도 (진짜 thrust 아닌 소견인)
- 양측 bilateral 시행
- 경추 arthrosis·고령 환자에 thrust 대체로 사용 가능

---

#### 3.4.6 Cervical Extension with Towel

![Cervical extension with towel (Fig. 47)](../images/states_manual/p50_cervical_extension_with_towel.png)

**원문 (p. 50)**
```
L:   Traction cervical spine. General technic for cervicals.
PP:  Supine.
DP:  At head of table facing caudad, in fencer's stance.
CH:  Grasps both ends of a towel which is rolled and placed under the
     base of the occiput, passed along both sides of the face, and
     twisted to prevent slippage.
IH:  Same (towel grip — both hands).
T:   Remove slack; apply gentle traction by pulling in a cephalic
     direction for 30–60 sec.
```

**한국어 요점**
- **도구(타월)를 이용한 sustained traction** — 환자 자가 시행도 가능
- 손이 작거나 접촉 어려운 경우·장시간 견인 필요시
- Thrust 없음 — 디스크 감압·근이완 목적

### 3.5 경추 Diversified 공통 경고 & 실무 정리

#### 🚨 절대 금기 (States Manual 기재 + Week 4 상세)
- 급성 whiplash injury (hyperflexion/extension 모두)
- 90° 경추 회전 불가 환자 (어느 방향이든)
- 경추 심한 골관절염, 경추 osteoporosis
- 경동맥 잡음·어지럼·5Ds 소견 (VBI 의심)
- 경성 콘택트렌즈 착용자 (Bilateral Thenar 등 안구 인접 접촉 기법 한정)

#### ✅ 선택 가이드라인 (States Manual의 관행 정리)
| 상황 | 1차 선택 |
|------|----------|
| 소아·소체형 RI–LI Atlas | **Lateral Atlas Index** |
| 중·대체형 RI–LI Atlas | **Lateral Atlas Pisiform** |
| C2–C7 PR/PL 기본 | **Rotary Cervical** (supine) |
| 임신·고령·supine 부담 | **Sitting Rotary Cervical** |
| 정밀 thrust 숙련자 | **Wrist Action Cervical Break** |
| 다중 분절 동시 | **Calcaneal Cervical Break** |
| 경추 arthrosis | **Bilateral Thenar Occiput** (thrust 생략) |
| Pre-adjustment 이완 | **Cervical Stretch Acromion** · **Cervical Extension with Towel** |

#### ⚠ 한국 임상 추가 고려
- **VBI 사전 스크리닝** (Week 2 Part 8 VBI cluster 참조)
- 초진 환자 1회 차는 **Rotary 회전 각도 최소**화 + IH dominance
- Adjustment 후 **즉시 재평가** (Week 2 Part VI: VOMS, Romberg) — 어지럼·두통 시 중단

## 제4부. 흉추 Diversified {#part4}

### 4.1 Upper Thoracic (T1-T4)

#### Prone — Double TP Contact
- 환자 prone, 머리 face piece
- 양손 hypothenar를 T3 양측 TP에 동시 접촉
- **P-A thrust** + 약간 inferior 성분

#### Seated Upper Thoracic
- 환자 seated, 검사자 뒤
- 양손으로 앞쪽 어깨 cross-arm 지지
- 체간 신전 + pre-load → P-A thrust

### 4.2 Mid Thoracic (T5-T8)

#### Prone Pisiform Contact
- 환자 prone
- 병변 분절 한쪽 TP에 pisiform
- 다른 손은 반대쪽 TP 안정화
- **P-A 방향** + listing 따른 각도

#### Knee-Chest Position (선택)
- 경추 굴곡 + 무릎 꿇기 자세
- T5-T8에 접근 개선
- 임산부·중증 고령자 회피

### 4.3 Lower Thoracic (T9-T12)

- Prone + side posture 모두 가능
- T10-T12는 요추와 유사한 접근 (mamillary process)
- Thoraco-lumbar junction (T12-L1)은 **unique** — 회전 방향 이동

### 4.4 Rib 교정

#### 1st Rib (upper)
- Seated 또는 supine
- Contact superior surface
- Cephalad + Anterior thrust

#### Mid Ribs (2-10)
- Prone, 병변 늑골 angle 접촉
- Lateral + Anterior thrust

**주의**: 골다공증 환자 늑골 골절 위험 — 신중한 force 조절

## 제5부. 요추 Diversified {#part5}

### 5.1 Lumbar Roll (측와위 — Gonstead-style)

**적응증**: L1-L5 분절 PL/PR/PLI/PRS 등
**환자 자세**:
- Side posture, 환측 위
- 아래 다리 신전
- 위 다리 굴곡
- 위 팔 자연스럽게 앞으로

**Set-up**:
1. 검사자 우측 pisiform → 환자 mamillary process (환측)
2. 좌측 팔뚝 → 환자 위 어깨 지지
3. 체간 rotation + lateral flexion pre-tension
4. Slack 제거 — elastic barrier 감각
5. **HVLA thrust** — listing 반대 방향

### 5.2 Prone Thrust

**적응증**: 측와위 불가 환자 (급성 통증·임산부 중기·고령)
**환자 자세**: Prone
**접촉점**: Spinous process 또는 mamillary
**LOD**: Posterior-Anterior + slight inferior

### 5.3 L5-S1 특이 기법

#### L5 PI (posterior-inferior)
- Side posture
- Ischial tuberosity 측 approach
- Superior-anterior thrust

#### L5-S1 Junction
- 디스크 탈출 흔한 부위
- 방사통 있으면 thrust 방향 신중
- Flexion-distraction (Cox-style)이 대안

### 5.4 Diversified vs Gonstead-style 요추

| 항목 | Diversified | Gonstead |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Listing 정밀도 | 낮음 (general) | 높음 (5 Components) |
| X-ray 의존 | 낮음 | 높음 |
| Bench 사용 | Regular table | Pelvic Bench |
| 접촉점 | Spinous or mamillary | Mamillary strict |
| Force | 중간 | 비교적 큼 |

→ 본 강의는 **Diversified**를 중심으로, **Gonstead 요소**(mamillary contact)는 **선택적** 통합.

## 제6부. 골반·천장관절 Diversified {#part6}

### 6.1 Listing 복습 (자세 세부는 Ch 4 Gonstead 참조)

- **R AS / L AS**: 장골 전·상방 회전 → LOD: P + I
- **R PI / L PI**: 장골 후·하방 회전 → LOD: A + S
- **EX (External flare)** / **IN (Internal flare)** 추가 수식

### 6.2 Pelvic Diversified — Side Posture

**R AS 예시**:
1. 환자 left side posture (우측 위)
2. 검사자 우측 pisiform → 환자 우측 PSIS
3. 좌측 팔 → 환자 우측 어깨 cross-support
4. Rotation pre-tension (환자 상체 반대로 회전)
5. **P-I thrust** (posterior + inferior)

**L PI 예시**:
- Right side posture (좌측 위)
- Contact: 좌측 ischial tuberosity
- LOD: A + S (anterior + superior)

### 6.3 Sacral Base 교정

- Prone, sacrum 전방 tilt
- Hypothenar contact on sacral base
- P-A thrust + 필요 시 측방 성분

### 6.4 SI Joint Gapping (alternative)

- Prone
- Ischium 외측 힘 → SI 관절 gapping
- 급성 SI 염증에서는 경도 mobilization만

## 제7부. 말초 관절 Diversified {#part7}

### 7.1 상지

#### 어깨 (Glenohumeral)
- Anterior-posterior mobilization (전방 탈구 방향 제한 환자)
- Long-axis distraction

#### Acromioclavicular (AC)
- 앙와위
- 쇄골 원위 anterior-posterior thrust

#### 팔꿈치
- Radial head — supine, 손목 supination + HVLA
- Ulnar nerve 증상 있으면 신중

#### 손목
- Carpals individual mobilization
- Distal radioulnar joint (pronation/supination)

#### MCP · PIP · DIP
- Individual joint long-axis traction + HVLA

### 7.2 하지

#### Hip
- Supine long-axis traction (HVLA 드뭄, mobilization 주로)
- Femoral anterior glide — 후방 탈구 제한

#### Knee
- Supine knee flex + extension barrier에서 HVLA
- Patella mobilization (medial-lateral, superior-inferior)

#### Ankle
- Talocrural — distraction + A-P
- Subtalar — eversion/inversion thrust

#### Foot
- Cuboid — "whip" technique
- Metatarsals — individual mobilization

### 7.3 악관절 (TMJ)

- Intra-oral contact (장갑 필수)
- Supine, 환자 입 벌린 상태
- Condyle distraction + 필요 시 lateral glide

**주의**: TMJ 교정은 치과·구강외과 영역과 겹침 — **협진** 원칙.

## 제8부. 술자 자기관리 — 손상 예방 {#part8}

### 8.1 Chiropractor Injuries (직업병)

- 손목 · 엄지 척측 측부 인대 부상
- 어깨 rotator cuff syndrome
- 요추 · 경추 퇴행
- Thumb · DIP osteoarthritis

### 8.2 Biomechanical Principle

- **체중 이용** — 팔 힘이 아닌 체간 drop
- **정렬** — 접촉손·팔·어깨·체간을 **일직선**으로
- **어깨 tuck** — 어깨 외전 최소 (mobilization 자세 수준 유지)
- **호흡** — 호흡 참고 thrust, 직후 호흡 재개

### 8.3 환자당 피로 관리

- 하루 교정 수 제한 (개인 차이, 30-50명/day)
- 20-25명 당 5-10분 휴식
- Warm-up stretches (어깨·손목·흉추)

### 8.4 Setup 최적화

- 높이 조절 가능한 테이블 (경추 vs 요추)
- 환자 긴장 풀기 (호흡 · 대화)
- 환자 자세 정확해야 술자 긴장 덜 함

### 8.5 장기적 경력 관리

- 매일 셀프 스트레칭 15분
- 주 1-2회 본인도 교정 받기 (상호 수기)
- 10년 시점부터 PT·의사와 협진 증가 (교정 수 감소, 진단·관리 시간 증가)
